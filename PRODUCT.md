# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A single Instagram creator reviewing publishing consistency and performance in short, deliberate check-ins. Their primary job is to see what was posted, understand how each reel performed over time, and avoid missing the next planned upload.

## Product Purpose
Reel Rhythm is a private tracker that syncs Instagram reels, records post dates and evolving analytics, and lays the publishing timeline into a calendar. It brings the answer to "what did I post, how is it doing, and what is next?" into one uncluttered workspace.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, encouraging. It should feel like an organised studio log rather than a noisy social-media dashboard.

## Anti-references
Avoid feed-like endless scrolling, gamified creator dashboards, busy notification surfaces, and ornamental analytics widgets that distract from the posting timeline.

## Design Principles
- Start with the publishing calendar, because cadence is the central habit.
- Keep the reel itself as the unit of attention: one click reveals its story over time.
- Make deadlines noticeable without turning the whole workspace into an alarm.
- Turn metrics into legible comparisons, not a wall of numbers.
- Keep integrations operationally clear: users should see freshness, connection state, and the next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specialised requirements were requested. Use predictable controls, readable labels, adequate contrast, and avoid motion that is required to understand a state change.
